# The Spooky Crime Scene

**Play it in your browser:** https://the-spooky-crime-scene.vercel.app

A first-person murder-mystery game made in Unity 6 (URP). Three cases, each with its own location, suspects, red herrings and twist. Search for evidence, question two shady informants, and make your accusation on the Case Board. You only get three tries.

## The cases

Cases unlock in order. Your best rank for each one is saved.

| # | Case | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The 3:15 Escapement** | A clocktower gear room at 3:15 AM. The inventor Arthur Vance lies beneath the fallen pendulum. |
| 2 | **The Lighthouse at Gull Point** | A storm-lashed lamp room. The light went dark, a silver ship hit the rocks, and the keeper lies at the foot of the stairs. |
| 3 | **The Nightshade Conservatory** | A moonlit glasshouse, locked from the inside. A botanist lies dead beside an orchid that blooms only at midnight. |

## Features

- 8 or 9 clues per case, including clues you can only examine in place, red herrings, and a final twist question
- A Case Board with a 4-question accusation and 3 attempts. A wrong accusation only tells you how many answers were right.
- Two informants in every case:
  - **Wren**, a spy who sells direct tips (each one costs your top rank)
  - **Finch**, a messenger kid who gives free riddles about the clues you're missing
- An intro card for each case, a case-select menu with saved progress, and detective ranks with good and bad endings
- Atmosphere:
  - **Clocktower:** moonlight through a clock face
  - **Lighthouse:** a storm with lightning and a rotating Fresnel lens
  - **Greenhouse:** a moonlit glasshouse with fireflies
- A procedurally synthesised horror soundscape for each location (wind, rain, thunder, waves, crickets, creaks, bells, whispers, a heartbeat), with no audio files

## Running it

1. Open the project in **Unity 6000.3** (Universal Render Pipeline, Input System).
2. Open `Assets/Scenes/MainMenu.unity` and press Play, then click inside the Game view.
3. To jump straight to a later case while testing, use **Tools > The Spooky Crime Scene > Unlock All Cases (testing)**.

## Rebuilding the content

- **3D models:** every model is generated by Blender scripts in `SourceArt/` (`build_clocktower.py`, `build_lighthouse.py`, `build_greenhouse.py`, with shared helpers in `meshkit.py`):
  ```
  blender --background --factory-startup --python SourceArt/build_lighthouse.py
  ```
- **Case text:** clues, riddles, tips, suspects, questions and solutions live in `Assets/Editor/CaseLibrary.cs`, which writes the `CaseDefinition` assets in `Assets/Cases/`.
- **Scenes:** in Unity, use **Tools > The Spooky Crime Scene > 2. Build Everything** to rebuild the menu and all case scenes.
- **Web build:** use **Tools > The Spooky Crime Scene > 3. Build WebGL (for Vercel)**. It writes `Builds/WebGL` together with `Deploy/vercel.json`, which serves Unity's Brotli-compressed files with the right headers.

## Project layout

- `Assets/Scripts/`: gameplay (player, inspection, case file, Case Board, dialogue, informants, intro card, menu, soundscape, storm)
- `Assets/Editor/`: the case library and the scene and web builders
- `Assets/Cases/`: the case data assets and the case catalog
- `Assets/Models/`: generated FBX models for each location
- `Assets/WebGLTemplates/`: the full-window web page with its themed loader
- `SourceArt/`: the Blender generator scripts and `.blend` sources
